# 客户沟通消息模板(全流程 · 英文可直接用)

> 从首次接触到交付、要好评的每个环节的英文话术。`[ ]` 处替换。中文是说明。
> 你只能书面英语——这些模板**全是文字沟通**,正好发挥你的优势。

---

## 1. 主动接触(LinkedIn / 邮件 / 平台外)
> Hi [Name], I saw [you're building / your post about] [X]. I'm a senior full-stack & AI engineer (19 yrs) who builds production RAG/LLM and full-stack apps end-to-end. I recently shipped an enterprise AI platform (https://aidooo.com). If you're looking for help with [their need], I'd be glad to share how I'd approach it — no pressure. Either way, best of luck with [X]!

**中文**:冷启动别硬推销;先夸他、点出你能帮的点、给链接、留余地。

---

## 2. Proposal 后跟进(投标 2–3 天没回复)
> Hi [Name] — just following up on my proposal for [project]. I know you're reviewing options, so to make it easy: I can start with a small paid milestone so you can verify quality risk-free. Happy to answer any questions here in chat. Would a quick written Q&A work for you?

---

## 3. 需求/范围确认(接单前对齐,防扯皮)
> Thanks [Name]! Before we start, let me confirm the scope so we're aligned:
> **Goal:** [one sentence]
> **Deliverables:** [bullet list]
> **Out of scope (for now):** [bullet list]
> **Tech:** [stack]
> **Milestones:** 1) [x] — $[ ] · 2) [y] — $[ ]
> **Timeline:** ~[ ] for milestone 1
> Does this match what you expect? Anything to add or remove?

**中文**:**接单前一定文字确认范围**——这是远程接单防纠纷最重要的一步,也降低退款/差评风险。

---

## 4. 启动 / Kickoff
> Great, let's start 🚀 To kick off I'll need: [access/credentials/sample data/repo]. I'll set up the project and deliver the first working slice by [date]. I'll post a short progress note each [day/2 days] here so you always know status — I work async, so no meetings needed unless you want them.

---

## 5. 进度更新(每天/隔天一条,建立信任)
> Update [date]: ✅ done — [items]. 🔜 next — [items]. No blockers. (Or: I need [X] from you to continue.) On track for [milestone] by [date].

**中文**:**日更短消息**是远程拿 5 星和 Top Rated 的关键——让客户全程安心、有记录。

---

## 6. 交付 / Handover
> Milestone [n] delivered ✅
> **What's included:** [list]
> **How to run:** [link to README / steps]
> **Demo:** [link / screenshots / short video]
> Please take a look and let me know if anything needs adjusting — happy to iterate. Once you're satisfied, you can release the milestone.

---

## 7. 要好评 / Testimonial(结单后)
> Thanks for a great project, [Name]! It was a pleasure building [X] for you. If you're happy with the work, a short review would mean a lot — it really helps me on the platform. And if you ever need [maintenance / new features / another project], I'm one message away. 🙏

**中文**:好评对新号是生命线;结单满意时**主动、礼貌**地要,大多数满意客户会给。

---

## 8. 异议 / 难题应对(英文话术)

**口语/视频面试要求:**
> I do my best work async and communicate in clear writing — I can answer any technical questions right here in detail, and I'm happy to record a short screen-share walkthrough of relevant work. Would written Q&A or a recorded demo work for you?

**时区(GMT+8):**
> I'm in GMT+8, but I keep flexible hours and reply within [a few hours]. Async updates mean progress continues across time zones — you'll often wake up to completed work.

**砍价:**
> I understand budget matters. My rate reflects 19 years of experience and end-to-end delivery (you get architecture, code, and deployment from one person). I can fit the scope to your budget — e.g., start with [smaller milestone] — rather than lower the quality bar. Would that work?

**付款安全(客户担心):**
> Let's use [Upwork escrow / milestone payments] so it's safe for both of us — you only release when you're satisfied with each milestone.

**NDA / 数据隐私:**
> Happy to sign an NDA. For sensitive data, I can also build with a **self-hosted model** so nothing leaves your infrastructure — that's a core part of what I do.

**改需求 / scope creep:**
> Great idea! That's a bit beyond our current milestone scope — I can add it as a small follow-up milestone of $[ ]. Want me to include it?

---

## 9. 礼貌拒绝 / 不合适的单
> Thanks for considering me, [Name]. This one isn't quite the right fit for my skill set, so I want to be upfront rather than overpromise. I'd be glad to help if you have [relevant work] in the future. Best of luck!

**中文**:不接的单也好好回——口碑和未来机会都靠这个。
